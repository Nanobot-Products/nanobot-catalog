Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Double Layer and Single Layer bottles?

Double Layer (Vacuum) bottles have two stainless steel walls with a vacuum insulation layer that maintains liquid hot for 18h or ice cold for 24h with zero sweat. Single Layer bottles have a single stainless steel wall designed for fast chilling inside refrigerator doors.

What is the difference between MRP and DP Price?

MRP (Maximum Retail Price) is the standard consumer retail price. DP (Dealer Price) is the discounted factory rate for authorized dealers, retailers, corporate gifting, and bulk buyers.
