TRUE VACUUM TECHNOLOGY
Our true vacuum technology encompasses two chamber high vacuum insulation chambers with very high performing multiple pumps to 
evacuate air and achieve high degree of vacuum insulation.
Insulation works on the principle of resisting three modes of heat transfer. Heat loss due to convection and conduction is prevented by vacuum 
insulation. Heat transfer caused by radiation is controlled dual technology of electrolysis and copper coating.
